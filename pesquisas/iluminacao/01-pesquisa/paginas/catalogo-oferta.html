05Páginas institucionais (política de frete, devolução, privacidade)

Estado: rascunho. Prazo e devolução seguem o padrão observado nos concorrentes americanos: 30 dias para devolver (Lightscordless; a Popular Lighting dá 100), frete grátis nos EUA (Stockholm-Lights) e arrependimento com frete de volta pago pelo cliente (Popular Lighting). Envio de armazém americano em 3 a 7 dias úteis.
Contact (contact)

Questions about your order, your lamp's battery or a gift? Write to hello@softnook.com and a real person answers within 1 business day, Monday to Friday. Please include your order number if you have one.

Shipping Policy (shipping-policy)

We ship free to every address in the contiguous United States. Orders are processed within 1 to 2 business days. Items stocked in our US warehouse arrive in 3 to 7 business days; if an item ships from our international partner warehouse, the delivery estimate (7 to 12 business days) is shown on the product page before you pay. Prices never include hidden import fees: if an item ships from abroad, any duty is paid by us, not by you at the door. Sales tax is calculated at checkout according to your state. You receive a tracking link by email as soon as your order ships.

Refund Policy (refund-policy)

You have 30 days from delivery to return any item for a full refund. Items must be unused and in the original packaging; request your return at hello@softnook.com before sending anything back. If the item arrived damaged or defective, send us a photo and we cover the replacement or the return label. For change-of-mind returns, return shipping is paid by the customer. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method within 10 business days. Gift cards and gift boxes are not returnable.

Privacy Policy (privacy-policy)

Softnook collects only the information needed to process and deliver your order (name, address, e-mail, phone) and, if you agree, to send you e-mails about new products and offers. Payments are processed by Shopify Payments and its partners; we never store your card details. We do not sell your personal information. You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data at any time at hello@softnook.com. California residents have the rights described in the CCPA, which we honor for all US customers.

Terms of Service (terms-of-service)

By using softnook.com you agree to these terms. Prices are in US dollars and may change without notice; the price that applies is the one shown at checkout. Product photos show the item as accurately as possible, but light tone may vary slightly by screen. Battery life is stated at medium brightness and decreases over time with charging cycles, as with any rechargeable battery. These terms are governed by the laws of the state where Softnook is registered.
