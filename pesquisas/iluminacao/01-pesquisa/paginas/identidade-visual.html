O kit visual da Softnook

Paleta, 4 prompts de identidade de marca, banners e um prompt por imagem de referência baixada, produto por produto.

Como usarPaletaIdentidade de marcaBannersSequência da PDPCarrosséis por produto

00Como usar

  1. Escolha a posição (logo, banner ou imagem de produto).
  2. Localize o arquivo de referência na pasta "Referencia de imagens - cena e prompt".
  3. Anexe a referência de cena + as fotos reais do produto (Product Master).
  4. Copie o prompt em inglês.
  5. Cole no ChatGPT ou Gemini e gere.
  6. Confira: o produto tem que sair idêntico à amostra.
Hierarquia: a referência manda na cena; o Product Master manda no produto.
Status: referência de produto pendente até a amostra chegar. Não gere imagem de produto antes disso.

01

Wordmark / logo

logo principal Softnook, versao espresso pra fundo creme e versao creme pra fundo espresso
Referência: gerar primeiro o wordmark e anexar nos seguintes
Prompt profissional em inglês (5 blocos)
REFERENCE HIERARCHY
No competitor reference is used for this asset. The only references are the Softnook palette below and the brand voice: a calm, warm American home in the evening, one soft lamp glowing in a reading nook. Do not imitate any existing lighting brand logo, and do not copy the typography of Lights Stockholm, Popular Lighting, Kiyo Home, Lightscordless or Creative.Lighting.

ART DIRECTION
A refined typographic wordmark reading exactly SOFTNOOK in a soft high-contrast serif close to Cormorant Garamond Medium, lowercase or small caps, generous letter-spacing. The two letter O may be drawn slightly rounder, as if lit from inside, but the mark must stay readable without any effect. The feeling is quiet, hand-made and premium, never techy, never neon, never playful cartoon.

BRAND FIDELITY
N/A for physical product. Colours: espresso #3A2E2A on cream #F7F2EB for the main version; cream #F7F2EB on espresso #3A2E2A for the reversed version; amber #C9823A only for an optional tiny glow accent. No gradients, no 3D, no drop shadows, no metallic effect.

BRAND AND LEGAL CLEANUP
No trademark symbols, no slogans, no extra words, no stock-logo shapes (no generic bulb icon, no house icon, no moon cliche). The spelling must be exactly S-O-F-T-N-O-O-K, checked letter by letter, with no space between soft and nook.

QUALITY CONTROL
Vector-clean edges, even kerning, readable at 120px wide on a phone. Deliver on transparent background, horizontal lockup, 3000x1000px PNG, plus the reversed version. Reject any output with misspelling, blurred strokes or decorative swashes that hurt legibility.

Ícone / favicon

monograma/icone para favicon e foto de perfil
Referência: gerar primeiro o wordmark e anexar nos seguintes
Prompt profissional em inglês (5 blocos)
REFERENCE HIERARCHY
Use the approved Softnook wordmark (generate it first) as the only reference for letterform style. No competitor icon is referenced. The icon must feel like the same family as the wordmark at a glance.

ART DIRECTION
A simple monogram built from a lowercase s inside a soft arch, the arch suggesting a cozy nook or the curve of a lampshade. One single idea, no more than two shapes. It must read clearly at 32x32px as a browser favicon and at 180x180px as an Instagram and Facebook profile picture.

BRAND FIDELITY
N/A for physical product. Cream #F7F2EB symbol on an espresso #3A2E2A circle for the profile version; espresso symbol on transparent for the favicon. Amber #C9823A allowed only as a 1px inner glow line, optional.

No letters other than the s, no bulb, no flame, no house, no star, no text. Do not resemble any existing lighting or furniture brand monogram.

QUALITY CONTROL
Perfectly centred, optical balance checked inside a circle crop. Deliver 1024x1024px PNG (profile) and 512x512px PNG transparent (favicon source). Reject outputs with thin hairlines that disappear at small size.
