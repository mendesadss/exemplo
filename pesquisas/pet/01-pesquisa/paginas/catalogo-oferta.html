05Páginas institucionais

Estado: rascunho. Prazo, garantia e tom seguem o padrão observado nos concorrentes (7 a 15 dias de entrega, 100 dias na MilaGift, frete grátis acima de $59 na Pet Lovers Custom) e foram calibrados para uma promessa que dá para cumprir.
Contact

Questions about your portrait, your order or a remake? Write to support@brushedpaws.com and we answer within 1 business day. Please include your order number and the pet's name.

Shipping Policy

Every Brushed Paws item is made to order. Production takes 3 to 5 business days after you approve the preview. Shipping to the United States takes 5 to 9 business days, so most orders arrive in 8 to 14 business days in total. Orders over $59 ship free. You receive a tracking link by email.

Why this window: competing pet gift stores in our research declare 7 to 15 days from order to delivery. We prefer to promise a number we can keep.

Refund Policy

You have 60 days to tell us if something is wrong. If the portrait does not look like your pet, we remake it for free. If you still are not happy, we refund the order. Because every item carries a name, we cannot accept returns of a correct product that you simply changed your mind about; we will always remake or refund first.

Why this design: customers in this market complain most about likeness and about being offered a refund when they wanted a remake.

Privacy Policy

We collect your name, email, shipping address and the photo of your pet only to make and deliver your order. We never sell your data. Photos are deleted from our systems 90 days after delivery. You can ask us to delete your data at any time.

Terms of Service

By ordering you confirm you own the photo you upload or have permission to use it. Colors on screen may differ slightly from the printed item. Production and delivery estimates are not guarantees. These terms are governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, United States.
